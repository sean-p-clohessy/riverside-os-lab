OPERATING SYSTEMS · UNIT 50 · LEVEL 5

Your self-guided OS lab

Two sessions of approximately two hours. You need a browser, somewhere to save your exports, and time to explain the evidence. No installation, physical controller or prior Linux experience is required.

Start and save

  1. Open your week. Quick start introduces the controls; you can reopen it at any time.
  2. Use Set name / alias to label your evidence. On a phone, open Menu for this control.
  3. Read the recap, then open the first investigation. Choose any desktop theme; the models are identical.
  4. Export progress before leaving or changing computers. Keep the JSON file. On another browser, Import progress restores it after confirmation.

Work saves only in this browser. Check the save message at the top. The tutor cannot see your work until you share your exported report through your normal course arrangements.

Use the three stages

  1. Learn & predict: read the explanation and key terms, then record your prediction. Being wrong is useful if the evidence helps you explain why.
  2. Experiment: follow every numbered run. Set up run selects the settings; you then press Run. Read the trace or results. The evidence notebook captures the required comparisons automatically.
  3. Explain & review: compare your prediction with the results. Reveal the worked debrief when ready. Write one or two supported paragraphs, select a conclusion, review your reasoning and choose Check investigation.

Use specific values or trace events. Explain the mechanism and a trade-off or limitation. “I clicked the right setting” is not enough. Reset model keeps your evidence; Restart this investigation removes that investigation’s local work after confirmation.

Finish and share your work

Complete all three investigations, then the five-question knowledge check. Read the feedback and retry any misconceptions. Finish the weekly reflection using your own results and check its self-review box.

The week status lists anything still needed. Each investigation has 100 practice points; completing the week also requires the knowledge check and reflection. These points record completion, not the academic quality of your writing.

In Session report, download the evidence report or print/save PDF. Also export progress JSON so you can continue later. Submit the readable report as your tutor directs. The app does not submit automatically.

If you get stuck

Use the diagnostic hint, set up the specified run again and inspect the worked debrief. Record your unresolved question in your explanation and ask your tutor. Source links are optional further reading and need internet access.

If browser storage is unavailable, keep exporting JSON while you work. If a saved record cannot be read, download its recovery copy before choosing Start fresh, or restore a valid JSON backup. Keep the original export until you have checked the restored work.

If the network fails, open the downloaded offline HTML in Edge or Chrome. It contains the explanations, models and checks. References and separately linked lecture files still need internet or their own downloaded copies.
